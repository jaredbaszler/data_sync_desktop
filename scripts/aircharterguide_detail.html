    Operating like carriers much larger in size, Air New England, upon its inception, has pledged to be the Northeast operator that others can only hope to emulate. Our commitment to safety and the integrity of the flight experience is second to none. With a fleet of twin-engine Beech Barons and a turboprop King Air, Air New England typically flies throughout the northeast, Eastern Canada and the mid-Atlantic region. However, our customers' excursions have had us venturing to the Midwest and deep South on occasion.

    Committed to quality control second to none, our maintenance program remains largely in-house allowing our seasoned mechanics to instantaneously address concerns before they become problems. Pre and post-flight inspections are the norm and what little mechanical outsourcing we do is through a single trusted partner well known as the industry leader in solving complex maintenance issues while committed to the same integrity standards that Air New England is accustomed to.

    Over the course of our existence, Air New England, has voluntarily subjected ourselves to a third-party safety vendor who audits our policies and procedures from an operational, training and mechanical standpoint. This is typically unheard of for a company of our size.

    Air New England’s commitment to our customers is unwavering from an operational, mechanical and experience standpoint. We are constantly sourcing additional aircraft to add to our fleet that may best fit our customers' needs. Our staff also sources aircraft through our sister company, Symphony Private Jets, if the situation warrants it. This allows us to provide the best, most suitable, cost-effective aircraft for the mission. Rest assured, there is no one better to partner with than Air New England for your air travel needs.
